## Bio

I obtained my B.S. and Ph.D. degrees from **Hefei University of Technology (HFUT)** in 2018 and 2023, respectively. My Ph.D. supervisor was **Prof. Liping Zheng**. I joined the **School of Public Safety and Emergency Management, Anhui University of Science & Technology (AUST)** in July 2023.

My research interests include **Computer Graphics**, **Computer Aided Geometric Design**, and **Emergency Management**. I am particularly interested in computational geometry-based modeling and optimization methods, especially **Power diagram-based algorithms** and their applications in facility location, capacity-constrained allocation, emergency evacuation, and intelligent emergency decision-making.
